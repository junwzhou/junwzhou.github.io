## English

On December 6, 2025, 27 team members gathered at East Lake Baima Station in Wuhan for an afternoon of outdoor activities. The program included dodgeball-style games, a team chase game, and a playing-card group challenge, encouraging communication, cooperation, and friendly interaction beyond regular research and study.

The gathering concluded with a group dinner, offering everyone time to relax, exchange ideas, and strengthen connections within the team.

<span id="zh"></span>
## 中文

2025年12月6日，27名团队成员相聚武汉东湖白马驿栈，开展轻松愉快的户外团队活动。活动设置了丢沙包、团队追逐和扑克牌抱团等互动环节，让大家在科研学习之外加强沟通协作，增进彼此了解。

户外活动结束后，团队成员共同聚餐，在轻松的交流中分享近况、畅谈学习与生活，进一步增强了团队凝聚力。
