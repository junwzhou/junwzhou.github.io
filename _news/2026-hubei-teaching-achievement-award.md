## English

The achievement "Development and Practice of a Data-Driven End-to-End Smart Teaching System for Programming Courses" received First Prize of the Hubei Provincial Teaching Achievement Award in the Higher Education (Undergraduate) category. Junwei Zhou was the second contributor among ten awardees.

## 中文

教学成果“全链条数据驱动的程序设计课程智慧教学体系创建与实践”获湖北省教学成果奖一等奖，成果类别为高等教育（本科）。周俊伟在10名获奖者中排名第二。
